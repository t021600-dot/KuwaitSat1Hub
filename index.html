SEC 02Reading this platform

Every panel declares what it is

A platform that mixes measurement with modelling has one obligation: never let a viewer confuse the two. Every chart, map and image on this site carries one of these four badges.

🛰 Measured

Published, attributable fact or imagery from KuwaitSat-1 and its project team. Source always linked.

🏛 Public data

Official open statistics — World Bank, WMO, Kuwaiti government publications. Figure, year and publisher shown.

▦ Reference dataset

Reference dataset, modelled on published Kuwaiti environmental patterns and used consistently across every panel.

◈ Modelled

Values produced by the platform's environmental model. Physically consistent, derived rather than measured.

⬡ Model output

Interpretation generated by the rule-based analyst in this page. Decision support, never a decision.

SEC Research console · for KuwaitSat-1 researchers Assisted workflow

State the objective. The agents do the legwork.

A researcher should not have to open every frame, zoom, squint and guess. Write what you are trying to find out, set the area and the period, and seven agents take it from acquisition through analysis, species selection, predicted impact, costed visualisation, post-deployment monitoring and a written report — each one showing its working and its sources.

The agents are assistants, not decision-makers. They operate only inside the scope you set, they stop at every gate that needs a person, and they never act on their own initiative.

SECThe wider system ⬡ Model output

An AI-powered environmental planning system

The question is not “where should we plant trees”. It is how Kuwait can use satellite technology, environmental data and AI to make its cities cooler, greener and more resilient to extreme heat. Greening is one instrument in that system — not the whole of it.

Recommendation → planning → monitoring

⬡ Agent workflow

A one-off recommendation is a guess that never learns. The value is the loop: every intervention becomes the baseline the next pass is measured against.

The platform does not simply say where to plant. It turns environmental data into a continuing cycle of analysis, planning, visualisation and monitoring — with a person deciding at every gate.

What the model actually looks at

Each input is labelled with what kind of data it is. Every figure names its origin. No panel presents a feed this platform does not carry.

SEC 13Orbit simulation ◈ Modelled — not live telemetry

Where Is KuwaitSat?

A simplified circular-orbit propagator running in your browser, initialised with the nominal sun-synchronous parameters of the Transporter-6 deployment. It is a teaching model: it does not read a TLE, it is not connected to a ground station, and the numbers below are computed here, not received from space.
