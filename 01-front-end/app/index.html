KuwaitSat-1 Mission Hub

A secure research platform for authorized KuwaitSat-1 researchers. Define a research question, select an area of Kuwait, and let a team of AI research assistants work through the analysis while you stay in control of every decision.

Researcher sign in

Access is restricted to authorized researchers. Missions, data and reports are private to the researcher who created them.

How it works

01

Define a mission

Write your research objective in plain language and draw the area of Kuwait you care about on the map.

02

Agents run

Six research assistants work through the steps in order, from pulling satellite scenes to drafting the report.

03

You approve the report

Nothing is published until you have read the findings and pressed Generate Report yourself.

The research assistants

Each agent handles one part of the workflow. The agents do not control the satellite and do not make decisions on your behalf.
