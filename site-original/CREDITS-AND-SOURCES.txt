    https://doi.org/10.3390/su16124955

[16] Li H et al. (2024). Cooling efficacy of trees across cities is determined by background climate, urban morphology, and tree trait. Communications Earth & Environment 5:754
    https://doi.org/10.1038/s43247-024-01908-4
    182 studies across 110 cities: in arid climates maximum pedestrian-level air cooling reaches −9.3 °C, with minor night-time warming up to +0.4 °C.
