EARTH IMAGERY — PROVENANCE
==========================

The hero plates are REAL satellite imagery. They are not drawings, and they
are not this platform's own model output. What stood here before them was a
canvas drawing — two ellipses standing in for the desert and the Gulf over a
blue gradient — and on a platform whose whole argument is that a reader can
see where a figure came from, the largest image on the page should not have
been invented.

WHAT WE DID TO IT
-----------------
Nothing was painted in and no surface detail was invented. The equirectangular
composite is sampled onto a sphere (ray -> sphere intersection -> lat/lon ->
bilinear texel), lit by a single directional source, given an atmospheric rim,
and photographed from an orbital viewpoint placed over 29.3 N, 47.7 E — so the
Gulf genuinely sits under the camera and Kuwait is genuinely at the centre of
frame. That is a reprojection of a photograph, which is why the plates are
badged as imagery rather than as a model.

The renderer is kept with the project so the plates can be regenerated or
re-aimed: see the notes in js/ — the hero reads the limb geometry out of the
plate rather than assuming it, so a re-render with a different viewpoint only
needs the PLATE constants updated.

WHAT IS STILL A MODEL, AND SAYS SO
----------------------------------
Drawn over the photograph, in the page's own colours: the orbit path, the
spacecraft, its swath cone and the Kuwait marker. Those are MODELLED. The
altitude, ground sample distance and swath quoted in the readout come from the
published KuwaitSat-1 record in the sources register; their arrangement on
screen is a diagram, not telemetry. No panel on this site carries live
satellite telemetry.
